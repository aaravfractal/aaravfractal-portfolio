Anon Wallet: the first five minutes

A privacy wallet with a browser extension and a mobile app. This teardown looks at what a new visitor meets before the first private transaction.

What works well

A clear promise on the homepage: practical privacy, wherever you need it. Release notes are dated and detailed. A "check your download" section explains how to verify the file. The Quick Start docs walk through account, funding, shielding and a private send. Version 0.11.1 added six languages.

What a first-time visitor meets

    Plain-words glossary

    Only terms Anon explains on its own pages.

    Shield

    Moving supported assets from your public balance into a private balance, so you can send and swap from it. Source: anon.inc homepage.

    Portal

    A full-page workspace that opens from your balance in the extension, for managing balances, swaps and positions. Source: anon.inc browser page.

    Network Guard

    Connects Anon to your VPN app. The site lists Mac now, with Windows and Linux coming soon. Source: anon.inc downloads.

    Railscan

    A page to explore public activity on Railgun. Source: anon.inc Products menu.

    .anon.buzz name

    A name people can find and pay, used as a username. Source: anon.inc Products menu.
